# Landing page (src/app/page.tsx)

Mode: Persuade. Audience: investors, researchers to hire, partners and data providers, weighted equally. Action: email the founders.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Markets are one connected system, so the page is one map. It refuses the category default of a dark page with a glowing node graph, eyebrows and three cards.

OWN-WORLD: A metro diagram. Paper ground (#F4F5F0), ink signage (#131A3A), five flat line colours (rates red, equities cobalt, credit amber, crypto green, futures purple). Overpass throughout, heavy for display. Actions are rectangular sign plates. Dashed track means planned.

STORY: The visitor sees assets as stations on lines, watches a shock travel through interchanges, reads that the graph model is still under construction, and emails the founders.

FIRST VIEWPORT: The map fills the viewport and its lines run off both edges. The title, pitch and primary plate sit in the map's open lower-left corner. An ink journey plate sits lower right with three origins to pick from and a marker riding the chosen route.

FORM: Transit map, pinned by Gabriel on 2026-11-18 from three presented directions ("Transit map"). seed: none (pinned) "Transit map (Recommended)".

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
