# Product

RavenGraph is a neolab building a world model of financial markets. This repo is the public website (ravengraph.com): a landing page, a notes section and one API reference page.

## Platform

web

## Stack

Next.js 16 (App Router, Turbopack), React 19, TypeScript, Tailwind v4 present but the landing page uses inline styles plus a few classes in `src/app/globals.css`. Deployed on Vercel.

## Users

Three groups, weighted equally (confirmed by Gabriel, 2026-11-18): investors, researchers the company wants to hire, and partners or data providers. All are finance or ML literate and will notice invented numbers.

## Product Purpose

Explain what RavenGraph is building, show honestly where it stands, and get the right people to email the founders.

## Positioning

"RavenGraph is a neolab building a world model of financial markets. Markets are one connected system: a rate move hits banks, then credit, then crypto. Our model learns that structure directly, across equities, futures and crypto, and gets better with every asset and link we add."

## Operating Context

Two founders. Mike Osorio (CTO), Gabriel Goulart (CEO). Contact is mailto:gabriel@ravengraph.com.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Engine: data pipelines, 80+ features, walk-forward validated models, in production for a year.
- Trading: live since February 2026.
- Partners: Avant Protocol (DeFi) and a LatAm stablecoin payments company.
- Graph model: in build. Current models are baselines.
- There are no real numbers to publish. No weights, lags, returns or benchmarks may appear unless Gabriel supplies them. Any performance number must carry "paper trading" and "small book" in the same line.

## Brand Commitments

- Copy rules: no em-dashes; no "it's not X, it's Y" constructions; never the word "intuition"; no "API", "pilot", "treasury", "corridor" or "savings" language in the hero, nav or CTAs.
- "Agents do research; validated models trade." Never imply an AI agent manages money.
- Tone: ambitious, confident, plain. Honest in the status section.
- The page must not look AI-generated (Gabriel rejected a dark card-and-eyebrow version and a dark single-column serif version).
- Brand colour is cobalt (see `docs/ravengraph-cobalt/`).

## Evidence on Hand

One published note in `content/notes/`. No charts, results or screenshots.

## Product Principles

Show the mechanism, do not assert it. Say what is in build.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA contrast, keyboard reachable controls, reduced-motion respected.
